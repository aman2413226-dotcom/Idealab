About IDEA Lab

Nurturing Innovation, Research & Entrepreneurship

IDEA Lab at Ajay Kumar Garg Engineering College serves as a collaborative innovation ecosystem where students transform ideas into real-world solutions through research, design, prototyping, startup incubation and interdisciplinary learning.

The laboratory integrates innovation, entrepreneurship, intellectual property awareness, product development and industry collaboration to encourage a culture of creativity and problem-solving among students.
